One sentence, 183 characters, pitching a bizarre startup idea that sounds absurd until you realise it would actually sell: fake paparazzi for micro-influencers. It sounds like a joke — paying strangers to ambush you outside a café so people think you are famous — until the money chain lands: brands only sponsor influencers who already look famous, so the look of fame is itself a product worth $99 a shoot.

Entry for make.fun's "Ridiculous at First Glance, Real by the Second" bounty ($2 in USDC). The program writes the pitch to `/output/entry.txt`; a judge model compares entries two at a time on how genuinely bizarre the idea is and whether the business logic survives a second look.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The pitch is written to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
